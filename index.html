FAQ
Is ZODA on mainnet?

No. The contract linked here is on BNB Smart Chain Testnet. A future mainnet deployment would use a different contract address.

How do I connect my wallet?

Select BNB Smart Chain Testnet in MetaMask and press Connect Wallet. Never share your recovery phrase or private key.

Can I buy ZDA here?

No. This website currently provides testnet information and wallet tools only. It does not sell tokens or provide real trading.

Is the token price or value guaranteed?

No price, market value, return or exchange listing is represented on this website.
